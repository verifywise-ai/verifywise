## VerifyWise Licensing FAQ

This FAQ explains the VerifyWise Community & Evaluation License (VCEL) 1.1 in plain language. It is not legal advice and it is not part of the license. Where they differ, [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md) governs. Section numbers below refer to it.

### What license does VerifyWise use?

The VerifyWise Community & Evaluation License (VCEL) 1.1. It is a source-available license, not an OSI-approved open-source license (Section 1). The copyright holder and Licensor is VerifyWise Ltd., registered in England and Wales and in Canada (Section 2.1).

### Does VCEL cover everything in the repository?

No. Parts that carry their own license file or declaration, such as the client SDKs, are covered by that license instead (Section 1). Third-party components keep their own licenses (Section 18).

### Who can use VerifyWise for free?

- Individuals, for personal use in a personal capacity (Section 4.1)
- Anyone using it for training and demonstrations, including paid courses and workshops, within the limits of Section 4.2
- Any organization, for a 60-day evaluation (Section 4.3)
- Anyone developing and testing a contribution to VerifyWise (Section 4.4)

Everything else needs a license.

### What changed from VCEL 1.0?

VCEL 1.0 let schools, universities, government and public-sector bodies, noncommercial research organizations and their contractors use VerifyWise free, including in production. VCEL 1.1 removes those free categories. Use by any organization now needs a separate written license, whatever kind of organization it is (Section 5).

### We are a university, a government body or a nonprofit. Do we need a license?

Yes. VCEL 1.1 treats every organization the same way: companies, government and public-sector bodies, schools, universities, research institutes, nonprofits, charities and NGOs (Section 2.4). Being public, charitable, nonprofit or publicly funded does not make your use free (Section 5). You can evaluate VerifyWise for 60 days first, and you can use it for training (see below).

### Can a teacher or trainer use VerifyWise in a course?

Yes. Training Use is free for individuals and organizations, and you may charge for the course (Section 4.2). Use sample, synthetic or fictional data. A training setup must not be used to run real governance, risk or compliance work for anyone, and must not become a production system later.

### Can a student use VerifyWise?

Yes, for personal learning in a personal capacity (Section 4.1), and as an attendee in a course under Section 4.2. Research or projects carried out for or on behalf of a school, university or research institute need a license (Section 5).

### Can my company run VerifyWise internally?

Only for evaluation, unless you have a license. Internal use by any organization is Organizational Use, even when it is self-hosted, used only by employees and generates no revenue (Section 5).

### Can my organization evaluate VerifyWise first?

Yes. Any organization may evaluate it for up to 60 consecutive calendar days, once per organization and its affiliates for each major version (Section 4.3). Evaluation must not replace an operational governance or compliance system, or be relied on for real regulatory, audit or contractual obligations.

### Can we host VerifyWise for our customers or sell it?

Not under VCEL. SaaS, hosting, managed services, white-label platforms, resale and customer-facing governance or compliance services all need a commercial license (Sections 5 and 6).

### Can a contractor install and run it for an organization?

Only for that organization's evaluation or training use. Installing, configuring or maintaining VerifyWise for an organization's production use needs a license (Section 5).

### A few of us work on VerifyWise together in our spare time. Are we an organization?

No. Individuals who collaborate informally on noncommercial development or contributions are not an Organization, as long as none of them is acting for one (Section 2.4).

### Can I fork or modify VerifyWise?

Yes, for permitted uses. Public forks are allowed for noncommercial development, experimentation, personal research, bug fixing and contributions. Forks and redistributions must keep the license and copyright notices. A fork does not widen the permitted uses: it may not be used by an organization, or be commercially hosted, sold or rebranded, without authorization (Section 7).

### Does a version ever convert to an open-source license?

No. VCEL has no automatic conversion to an open-source license (Section 19).

### We already use an earlier VerifyWise release. What changes?

Nothing for that release. Rights validly granted under earlier licensing arrangements, including the Business Source License and VCEL 1.0, stay governed by those arrangements for the versions released under them (Section 19). VCEL 1.1 applies to versions released under it.

### Do contributors need to sign anything?

The Licensor may require a Contributor License Agreement before merging a contribution (Section 8). Running and modifying VerifyWise to develop and test a contribution is a permitted use, even when you do it for your employer (Section 4.4). It does not cover any other use by your employer.

### What about trademarks?

VCEL grants no trademark rights. You may say truthfully that a product is compatible with or integrates with VerifyWise, but you may not claim endorsement or use the marks in a confusing way (Section 10).

### How do we get a license?

Contact [VerifyWise](https://verifywise.ai/contact). Rights beyond the permitted uses come only from a signed written agreement. Asking for pricing does not grant them (Section 12).
